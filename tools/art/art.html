Four things it does in the side panel

How it works

One email in, one useful answer out

1

Open an email

The add-in wakes up beside the message you are reading, in Gmail or Outlook.

2

It reads the email

Only the open message, or its thread, is read through the Gmail or Outlook add-in API.

3

Send it to OpenAIonly network call

The email text goes to the OpenAI API, using the key you saved in the add-in's Settings.

4

Shown in the panel

A summary, an editable reply draft or a chat answer. Nothing is sent until you choose.

Under the hood
Gmail · Email Buddy (Apps Script)
    1 triggercontextual trigger on any open message 2 readGmailApp: message, or last 25 in the thread 3 modelgpt-4o-mini via UrlFetchApp 4 outCardService cards: summary, reply, chat
Outlook · AI assistant + summarizer (Office.js)
    1 triggerribbon button opens the task pane 2 readmailbox.item.body.getAsync(text) 3 modelgpt-3.5-turbo, key-points then summary 4 outtask pane view + displayReplyAllForm

The Next.js summarizer is the early, offline version: it picks the first sentences and makes no AI call.
